How you use it

Give your agent the skill. Then give it the truth.

This is not a design app with a blank canvas. It is a workflow your AI agent follows, turning a product brief into decisions and editable brand assets.

01 / Add it

Make the skill available to your agent.

Add this repository or its adapter to Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, or another compatible agent environment.

Read the setup notes ↗
02 / Brief it

Start with the product—not visual preferences.

Tell the agent what you are building, who it helps, the promise, proof, alternatives, and any name or existing identity.

Use $tech-branding-logo to develop a brand for [product].
03 / Shape it

Make decisions in stages.

The agent first chooses a route: zero-to-brand, name-to-brand, or brand evolution. It then shows the foundation and directions before delivering assets, rules, and QC evidence.

What leaves the session

Not just a mark. A point of view your product can keep using.

The skill connects the decisions that most branding tools leave disconnected: what you build, who it helps, how it should feel, and how it should look.

The method

Creative range, then hard decisions.

This is a workflow for agents that need to make taste accountable—not a button that spits out a brand kit.

Brief the truth

Map the customer, job, promise, proof, category and the future the product needs to grow into.

Find the nouns

Translate abstract claims into visual raw material with surprising but useful associations.

Earn the mark

Generate broadly, score transparently, then refine selected directions for silhouette and use.

Test the system

Pressure-test the identity in favicons, interfaces, docs, dark mode, and real constraints.
