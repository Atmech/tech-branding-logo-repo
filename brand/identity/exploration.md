# Name-led exploration

The name and palette are fixed. This is an evolution of an editorial identity, so the study concentrates on lettering, an initial and framing. Nine studies across three genuinely different routes replace an unrelated fifty-symbol exercise. See `studies.svg` and `directions.svg` for editable boards.

## Useful nouns and their jobs

Considered: cut, counter, page, proofline, frame, stem, bowl, desk, card, edge, baseline, imprint, crop, aperture, route. Selected for this recommendation: **cut** (selection), **counter** (space that makes a letter recognizable), **stem** (structure), **card** (a practical work object), **edge** (hierarchy). These are designer-selected subjects, not owner-approved metaphors.

## Refined choice

The recommended b uses one even-odd filled path. Its 68-unit viewBox includes a solid 14-unit stem, a broad bowl and a 12-unit-high open counter. The diagonal is in the negative space so the identity survives without an accent color. In the lockup, the outlined lowercase wordmark is deliberately quieter than the mark.

The dedicated favicon has a 32-unit construction with a larger relative opening for 16px use. It is an optical small-size variant, not a new logo. The icon and color-free lockup were inspected in rendered previews. Familiarity of a lowercase b is a usability advantage and an originality limitation; no exhaustive comparison or legal screening has been performed.
