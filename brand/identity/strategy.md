# branded — the idea behind the identity

Recommended direction, 25 October 2026. This develops the owner-selected name and the paper/ink/lime/coral design the owner explicitly likes. The new mark and copy are recommendations for review, not recorded owner approvals.

## The real job

branded is an open-source skill that gives an agent a structured way to develop identities, campaigns and brand applications. It routes a brief, loads relevant expertise, preserves project decisions, and asks for editable outputs and checks appropriate to the surface. The CLI is an optional installation and project-memory utility. Actual asset production depends on the host's tools.

The primary audience assumption is a builder or small team already working with an agent: a founder, design engineer or hands-on operator who needs to give a product a clear public identity. The workflow also serves hospitality, property and apparel work; the homepage must make that breadth visible. An enterprise procurement story is unnecessary for this brief.

Their difficult moment is not merely “I need a logo.” It is “I have something to put into the world, and I don't know how to make the brand feel like the thing I believe in.” They may be carrying a temporary logo, too many aesthetic references, disconnected generated images, and a brief that disappears between chats. These are working audience hypotheses, not interview findings.

## The brand psychology

**Transformation:** from uncertain aesthetic choices to a direction the user understands and can continue developing.

**Brand role:** a creative partner who helps the user find the point, make choices and carry them into usable work.

**Core promise:** a clear point of view and a brand you can build on.

**Emotional benefit:** creative confidence rooted in understanding the decision. The target feeling is “I know what I'm making, and why.” It is an intended response, not a measured effect.

**Reasons to believe:** brief-first routing; selective domain modules; explicit exploration, refinement and QC; editable production files when supported; a decision log; portable `.branding/` state. These mechanisms already exist in the product. They are more credible than an instant-result claim.

**Personality tensions:** considered and expressive; candid and encouraging; structured and open to surprise. Each tension should affect behavior, not simply populate an adjective list.

**Behavioral principles:** ask for context before prescribing a style; explain what a choice helps the audience notice; explore alternatives before polishing; preserve approved work; disclose unsupported tools or untested surfaces. The user owns the identity and its decisions.

**Anti-positioning:** promises of an instant perfect brand, aesthetic output without reasoning, performative jargon, or a polished mockup presented as a usable production asset.

**Positioning:** For builders who need a credible brand and a way to make its creative decisions, branded is an open-source agent skill that connects product truth to a usable identity, campaign or application through a focused brief, selective expertise, editable work and project memory.

**Primary line:** Make it mean something.

**Descriptor:** Brand strategy, inside your agent.

**Supporting promise:** A clear point of view. A brand you can build on.

**Working principle:** Clarity before decoration. Character with a reason.

The short name is retained without inventing a story about its origin. Its descriptive breadth fits identities, campaigns and applications. Because it is a common word, pair it with the descriptor in discovery contexts. No name, trademark, domain or registry clearance is claimed.

## Before, during, after

| Moment | Desired feeling | Brand behavior | Evidence in the homepage/product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First encounter | “This understands the job I need done.” | Lead with a human outcome and a plain category descriptor. | Hero proposition, method and three work scopes. |
| Starting | “I can start with what I already know.” | Offer a focused first question and clear installation routes. | Native guide, actual local CLI command selector and example request. |
| Exploring | “There is room for my judgement.” | Present distinct directions, reasons and tradeoffs. | Direction board; skill preserves approvals and rejections. |
| Choosing | “I can explain why this is right for us.” | Connect the visual choice to product and audience, then test its use. | Strategy, considered-cut rationale, reproduction board and QC. |
| Returning | “The work is still mine, and we can keep going.” | Preserve context and show what has been decided. | `.branding/` state and decision log. |

These are causal design hypotheses. No universal claim that a hue, shape or font produces a fixed emotion is required. The aim is to reduce uncertainty through understandable choices and visible continuity.

## What the existing brand keeps, evolves and retires

| Element | Decision | Reason and confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `branded` name, repo URL, MIT license | Retain | Explicit owner decision and established project identifiers. High confidence. |
| Warm paper, ink, lime, coral | Retain | Explicitly liked by the owner. High confidence. |
| Oversized editorial serif, plain utility typography | Retain and refine | Central to the supplied visual reference; separates the big thought from the next step. High confidence in preference; audience response untested. |
| Worktable cards, hard offset edges, disciplined grid | Evolve | Preserve the recognizable visual language while making the hero a clear transformation rather than a list of commands. |
| Original T-like modular mark | Retire from current use; preserve in Git history | T belonged to the former name; there is no measured public recognition to justify retaining that letterform. The new name deserves a direct mnemonic. |
| Repeated developer-tool language as the entire story | Evolve | Installation matters, but the emotional reason to use the product is confident creative work. Keep commands in the installation section. |
| Unverified registry commands or invented social proof | Exclude | Product trust depends on instructions that match the actual distribution state. |

## Identity direction: the considered cut

The recommended symbol is a lowercase b, built as a single filled shape with one angled counter. Its substantial stem and bowl make it usable in small digital contexts; the internal cut introduces the editorial idea of selection. The name provides the immediate reading. The metaphor is supporting rationale, not something a user must decode.

Nine type-led studies were made across three distinct ideas: the considered-cut initial plus direct wordmark; an editorial proofline; and an open frame. This is a name-led evolution with a fixed palette and visual reference, so a focused lettering/initial study is more useful than fifty unrelated pictograms. All nine studies and three direction boards are included as editable SVGs.

The cut route wins on compact application, a direct relationship to the new name, one-color construction and extension into cards and image crops. The proofline is articulate but gives the product a more literary, publication-like voice; its underline loses usefulness as a standalone icon. The open frame is collaborative but resembles familiar editor brackets and becomes bulky in a navigation lockup. These are comparative design judgments, not market research.

## How the visual system earns its place

Paper gives the page breathing room and continuity with the owner's preferred design. Ink carries hierarchy and long reading. Lime gives a completed idea or active work area a clear visual territory. Coral introduces a small interruption at the proposition or edge. These are roles within this composition, not generic color-psychology facts.

Use Georgia for propositions, system sans for instructions and body copy, and mono for labels. The wordmark uses outlined letterforms and needs no client-side font. A slightly darker coral is used for large text on paper to meet the relevant contrast threshold; the original bright coral remains in surfaces and decoration.

The characteristic composition is an editorial desk: an oversized thought on one side, one illustrative work object on the other, and a grid that gives the work order. Essential instructions stay straight. Illustration can tilt, but does not carry the only copy a user needs.

## Proving the idea next

With five representative users, ask what branded does after a short homepage view, whether they can find a valid installation path, what the new mark reminds them of, and whether a sample direction gives them a reason to choose. Later, check recognition of the b among competing marks and whether resuming a project preserves useful context. Compare a matched task and environment before claiming improvement. Actual audience feedback, competitive visual screening and long-term recognition remain open.
