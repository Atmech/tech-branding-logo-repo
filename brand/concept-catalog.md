## Developed directions

### D01 — Constructive Blueprint (selected)

- **Concepts:** 01, 05, 19, 43, 50
- **Silhouette:** A broad top beam, centred stem, and two right-side planes balanced by a diagonal cut.
- **Story:** The system provides structure, while explicit judgement reveals a distinctive public expression.
- **Strength:** Best bridge between product rigour and creative craft; holds at favicon scale.
- **Risk:** The expressive colour version should not be the only version used.

### D02 — Signal Flag

- **Concepts:** 10, 11, 22, 29, 37, 49
- **Silhouette:** A vertical mast with a crisp signal panel breaking beyond it.
- **Story:** A founder gets a clear signal when a brand decision is ready to move.
- **Strength:** High energy for social and launch work; directional motion is built in.
- **Risk:** More familiar and more likely to be read as a flag, alert, or feature marker.

### D03 — Open Aperture

- **Concepts:** 04, 13, 16, 28, 30, 46, 48
- **Silhouette:** An open frame with an off-axis internal cut.
- **Story:** The brand agent frames the product truth so a distinctive route becomes visible.
- **Strength:** Excellent for product UI surfaces and document layouts.
- **Risk:** Less distinctive as a standalone mark; potentially associated with camera or design software.

**Selection.** D01 was selected because it scored strongest across distinction, small-size function, production readiness, emotional fit, and system extensibility. D02 remains a useful social-motion device; D03 informs the page's frame and card language.
